Cybersecurity Awareness

Practical education for everyone

Short, plain-language guidance on staying safe online.

Online security awareness

Keep devices updated, use official app stores, and think before clicking or sharing.

Network security concepts

Firewalls filter traffic, encryption protects data in transit (look for HTTPS), and segmenting networks limits damage. Avoid sensitive logins on public Wi-Fi without a trusted VPN.

Password security

Use long unique passphrases, a password manager, and two-factor authentication wherever offered. Never reuse passwords.

Phishing awareness

Be wary of urgent messages, mismatched sender addresses and unexpected attachments. Open the official site yourself rather than following the link, and report suspicious messages.

Vulnerability disclosure

If you find a flaw, report it privately to the owner or their security contact, give time to fix it, and do not publish exploit details first.

Responsible security research

Test only systems you own or have written permission to test. Unauthorised access is illegal, whatever the intent.

Digital privacy

Review app permissions, limit personal details on social media, and use privacy settings on accounts and devices.
